We welcome families interested in joining the Mrisho Gambo Secondary School community. Contact our administration for current places, application dates and the official requirements.

A clear path to joining our school

Whether you are asking about Form One placement or a transfer into another form, our school administration can guide you through the next steps and answer your questions.

Ask about admission
How to apply

The admission process

Make an enquiry

Contact the school to ask about the form you are applying for, available places, current dates and how applications are being received.

Confirm what to bring

Ask the administration for the current official checklist. They will let you know which records or forms are needed for your circumstances.

Submit your application

Follow the instructions provided by the school and submit the requested information to the administration for review.

Receive next steps

The school will explain the outcome, reporting arrangements and any remaining steps directly to the applicant or family.

Be prepared

Before you contact us

To help the administration advise you, have these details ready where applicable:

  • Student details: Full name, date of birth and the form being requested.
  • Previous school: Name of the school and the student's current or most recently completed form.
  • Academic records: Ask whether examination results, reports or a transfer letter are needed for this application.
  • Parent or guardian contact: A current phone number and other contact details requested by the school.

Please note: This is a preparation guide, not the official document checklist. Requirements can vary by form and application type. Confirm the current instructions with the school before making copies or travelling.

Common questions

How do I find out if there are places available?

Contact the school administration directly. Availability and application periods may change during the year.

Can I apply for a transfer into another form?

Ask the administration about transfer availability and the records they require to assess the request.

Where can I get the official application requirements?

Contact the school before applying. The administration can provide the latest instructions and tell you how to submit an application.
